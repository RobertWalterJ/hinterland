# Response to the 19 September audits

Two reviews ran in parallel: a feature audit (`2026-09-19-feature-audit.md`) and an adversarial design review (`2026-09-19-design-review.md`). They agreed on the main faults. This file records what was adopted, what was changed from the proposal, and what is deferred and why.

## Adopted and built

## Changed from the proposal

- **D15 (the allocation toggle on the phone)** was not removed. It still changes the phone's waterfall (competitive split into competitive and allocation); only the quadrant chart is desktop-only.
- **The bottom tabs** are Home, Compare, Map and Learn rather than "Place, Compare, Map, Learn". Robert already calls the first screen Home.

## Deferred, with reasons

- **Place-of-work jobs by industry before 2021, and commuting in 2016 (feature audit items 14 and 15).** These need new Statistics Canada tables and a concordance check. The catalogue numbers in the audit are marked "verify" and must not be used from memory.
- **Occupation and work-from-home change, 2016 to 2021 (items 12 and 13).** The data is in the raw 2016 file on disk; this needs a pipeline change and an NOC 2016/2021 concordance note.
- **Rank and peer comparison over time (item 9).** Next on the Change screen.
- **A picture in each lesson and a recap chip (M8).**
- **Tuning the learning pace (audit item 13).** It should be done against the simulator's guards rather than by eye.
- **Map population change for any period (D16).**
- **A desktop left rail (m10).** The desktop keeps its tab strip, relabelled in plain words.
